## 💡 Tips for Great Loading Animations

- Keep animations between **1–3 seconds** per loop.
- Avoid distracting users during long loading periods.
- Match the animation theme to your product or brand.
- Provide progress indicators when possible.
- Ensure accessibility by respecting reduced-motion preferences.
- Optimize SVGs and Lottie files for performance.
